Our Trips

Trips Information Requirements
Family Float Adventures Perfect for families looking to enjoy a relaxing day on the water. This gentle float trip allows participants of all ages to experience the beauty of nature while safely navigating calm waters. Family-friendly activities and picnic options are available.
  • Duration: 2 hours
  • Difficulty Level: Easy
  • Included: Safety gear, family-friendly guide, and picnic lunch.
  • Price: $50 per person
Extreme Rapids Challenge This thrilling adventure is designed for adrenaline seekers. Experience the most challenging rapids and navigate through intense water conditions with the guidance of expert instructors. Suitable for those with prior rafting experience.
  • Duration: 3 hours
  • Difficulty Level: Advanced
  • Included: Safety gear, experienced guide, and refreshments.
  • Price: $85 per person
Scenic River Escapes This trip is designed for those who want to immerse themselves in stunning landscapes while enjoying a moderate rafting experience. Paddle through picturesque rivers, observe wildlife, and learn about the local ecosystem from knowledgeable guides.
  • Duration: 4 hours
  • Difficulty Level: Moderate
  • Included: Safety gear, guide, and snacks.
  • Price: $65 per person
